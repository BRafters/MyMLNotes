- For our program to make a level of guess, we have to determine what a line would look like through those data points
- A Line is determined by its:
    - Slope
        - Is a measure of how steep the line is
    - Intercept
        - Is a measure of where the line hits the y-axis
- For each point y on a line, we can say
    - y = mx + b
    - M is the slope
    - B is the intercepts
    - Y is a given point on the y-axis, and it corresponds to a given x on the x-axis
- When we perform Linear Regression, the goal is to get the best slope and intercept for our data
